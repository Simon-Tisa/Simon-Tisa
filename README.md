### About me

I'm **Zhongguo Cai (Simon)**, an Electronic Information Engineering undergraduate at Jinan University. I study how machine learning models generalize across datasets, with a focus on medical imaging and physiological signals. At Foxconn, I worked on machine learning for manufacturing and on LLM applications with human review.

### Publication

**[A review of Kolmogorov–Arnold networks for medical image analysis](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compmedimag.2026.102827)**  
Co-first author · *Computerized Medical Imaging and Graphics*, 2026  
We reviewed 138 studies and examined how experimental design affects what we can conclude about KANs in medical imaging.
